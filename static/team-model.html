Choosing a model

$16,000 team cap or straight $12,000?

How the math works
  • Team model: the agent pays the team its SOI split until the team cap, plus the Zillow split on every Zillow deal. Out of that, the leader pays Real its 15% until the team member's Real cap ($6,000, or $4,000 once team volume passes $100M). The leader keeps the rest.
  • Real's 15% is figured on commission after the Zillow referral fee.
  • Independent model: the agent pays Real 15% until the $12,000 cap. The leader earns rev share on that company dollar: 33.33% on 1st line, 26.67% on 2nd, 20% on 3rd. At the full cap that is $4,000, $3,200 and $2,400 per agent.
  • Real requires a minimum count of capping front-line agents to unlock deeper rev share tiers. Confirm current unlock rules and fees with Real before presenting.
  • Post-cap fees are spread across the deals that close after the agent caps with Real.
  • Agents pay the C2 $39/month tech fee and their own TC, so the leader's costs stay near zero.
